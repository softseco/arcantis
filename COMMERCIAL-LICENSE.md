# Using Arcantis in production

Arcantis is licensed under the Business Source License 1.1. [LICENSE](./LICENSE) is what
counts; this page says the same in plain words.

- **Testing, development, devnet, research and security audits** are free for everyone, with
  no notice.
- **Production use with eUSD** (holding, sending, receiving or otherwise supporting eUSD, or
  another token Softseco issues) is free for everyone, whatever your size. You tell us that
  you use Arcantis (below).
- **Other production use** is free while the Total Finances of your company and its affiliates
  (the largest of revenue, budget and funding raised) were USD 5,000,000 or less over the last
  12 months. You tell us that you use Arcantis (below).
- **Above USD 5,000,000** you need a commercial license. Tell us within 30 days; you can go on
  using Arcantis for 90 days after that while we agree one. Softseco sets the terms of every
  commercial license, and may grant one at no charge, for example to a partner that supports
  eUSD.
- Production use that was not notified is not licensed, and we may ask for a commercial license
  covering the whole time it lasted.
- Each version becomes Apache-2.0 on its Change Date, four years after its release (for
  1.0.0-rc.1: 3 November 2030; for 1.0.0-rc.2: 5 November 2030).

## Telling us that you use Arcantis

Within 30 days of your first production use, write to **developers@softseco.com** with:

- your company's legal name (or your own name, if you use Arcantis as an individual);
- a contact address;
- the product or service in which you use Arcantis.

There is no fee and nothing else to fill in. We reply to confirm. Registered users also get our
security notices about Arcantis.

## Asking for a commercial license

Write to **developers@softseco.com** with your company's name, what you are building, and
roughly how you will use Arcantis. We reply to every request.

## What we do with what you send

Softseco (Joachim Szamborski) keeps your notice to know who uses Arcantis under its license, to
contact you about licensing, and to send security notices. We do not share it or use it for
marketing. We keep it while you use Arcantis and for three years after your last contact with
us, unless the law requires longer. You can ask to see, correct or delete it at
developers@softseco.com.
